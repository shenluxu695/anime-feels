Privacy Policy

Effective: May 8, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and basic profile information.

Social Media Data: When you connect YouTube or TikTok accounts, we collect:

  • From YouTube: channel information and video metadata necessary for publishing
  • From TikTok (via user.info.basic): basic profile information (username, avatar)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely for:

  • Operating and maintaining the Service
  • Publishing content you create to your connected social media accounts
  • Improving our AI video generation technology
  • Communicating about service updates

3. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS) and restricted API access.

4. TikTok Data Usage

When you connect TikTok via Login Kit, we request the following permissions:

  • user.info.basic: To verify your identity and display your TikTok username
  • video.upload: To publish videos you create directly to your TikTok account

We do not access, store, or share your TikTok data beyond what is necessary for these functions. You can revoke access at any time via your TikTok settings.

5. YouTube Data Usage

When you connect YouTube, we request access to publish videos to your channel. We do not read, modify, or store your YouTube data beyond what is required for publishing. You can revoke access via Google's security settings.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your data with third parties except as necessary to provide the Service (e.g., API calls to YouTube and TikTok).

7. Data Retention

We retain your account data until you delete your account. Generated videos are stored for 30 days unless you choose to keep them longer.

8. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us.
